Reflex Party

Each player gets one button. Every round has a rule. Press at the right moment to score; press too early or at the wrong moment and you lose a point. First to 5 wins.

Players

Buttons

  • Blue Q · Orange P · Green Z · Pink M
  • On a phone or tablet, each player taps their own corner of the screen.
  • Controllers: each player presses A on their own controller (controller 1 is Blue).
