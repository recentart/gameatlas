Block Drop

Falling blocks: move and turn each piece so it fills whole rows. Full rows clear and score points; clear several at once for more. The game ends when the pile reaches the top.

Starting speed

Controls

  • Keyboard: ← → move, ↑ or X turn, Z turn back, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop.
  • Controller: D-pad moves and drops, A turns, B hard drops.
  • Touch: the buttons under the well.
