Mine Field

Reveal every safe square without hitting a mine. A number tells you how many mines touch that square. Flag the squares you think hide mines. Your first reveal is always safe.

Size

Controls

  • Click to reveal, right-click to flag. Touch: tap to reveal, long-press to flag, or switch the Tap button to Flag.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter reveals, F flags.
  • Controller: D-pad moves, A reveals, X flags.
