Tank Duel

Take turns lobbing shells across the hills. Set the angle and power, allow for the wind, and fire. Shots blast craters in the ground. The last tank standing wins.

Tanks

People playing (computer drives the rest)

Controls

  • Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, Space fire. Players take turns on one keyboard.
  • Controller: stick or D-pad to aim and set power, A to fire.
  • Touch: the buttons at the bottom.
