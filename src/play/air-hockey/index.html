Air Hockey

Knock the puck into the other goal. First to 7 wins. Great on a tablet with a friend: each of you drags a mallet on your own half.

Mode

Computer

Controls

  • Touch: drag your mallet (left half is Blue, right half is Orange).
  • Mouse: move Blue. Keyboard: WASD Blue, arrow keys Orange.
  • Controllers: left stick, one per player.
