Brick Breaker

Bounce the ball off your paddle to clear every brick. Where the ball hits the paddle sets its angle. Three lives; each level is a little faster.

Difficulty

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: move the paddle; click or tap to launch.
  • Keyboard: ← → or A D, Space to launch.
  • Controller: stick or D-pad to move, A to launch.
